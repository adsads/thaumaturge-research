Thaumaturge Research Helper

Drag aspects from the right onto your research note, and the gaps fill in automatically. Classic From/To finder

    Drag an aspect onto a cell. Click an empty cell to toggle a hole. Click an aspect on the grid to remove it, or drag it to move it (drop it off the grid to remove).

    Aspects

    Drag to place. Click to disable ones you haven't unlocked (they're only used when there's no other way).
